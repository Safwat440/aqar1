How It Works

Four Clear Steps

The same path applies to auctions, brokerage and marketing — the details differ, the order doesn't.

01

Request & Assessment

You send your requirements or property details, and the team reviews the legal status, title deed and market value.

02

Preparation & Documentation

Preparing the terms booklet or offering file, setting the sale mechanism, participation terms and dates.

03

Listing & Marketing

Publishing the auction or project, and managing inquiries and viewings up to the sale date.

04

Award & Title Transfer

Completing payment and transfer procedures with the relevant authorities, and delivering documents to the new owner.
