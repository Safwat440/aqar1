Real Estate Valuation & Advisory

A written technical opinion on market value and best use of the asset, that precedes a sale or investment decision — not one that follows it.

Register Your RequirementsContact Us

Exactly What We Handle

  • Market Value Opinion

    Estimating a price range for the asset based on registered comparable transactions in the same area and site characteristics.

  • Best Use of the Asset

    Comparing legally permitted use alternatives, and the effect of each alternative on value and return.

  • Preliminary Feasibility Study

    Estimating cost, revenue and duration for a proposed project, with conservative and moderate scenarios.

  • Regulatory Risk Review

    Reading the title deed, restrictions and encumbrances, and stating what needs addressing before listing or purchase.
