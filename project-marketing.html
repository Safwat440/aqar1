Exactly What We Handle

  • Market Research

    Analyzing competing supply and absorption rate within the project area, and defining the target segment.

    A pricing schedule per unit type, and a phased launch that protects price and maintains sales pace.

  • Sales Identity & Materials

    Project messaging, the offering booklet, marketing layouts, and point-of-sale materials.

    Digital and field campaigns, lead qualification, and weekly cost-per-qualified-lead tracking.

    A field team on site, and periodic performance reporting to the developer with sales and inventory figures.
