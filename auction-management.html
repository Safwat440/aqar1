Service One

Real Estate Auction Management

We run the auction as a neutral party: preparing the file, announcing the terms and running the session in front of everyone with the same information, then awarding and documenting the result.

Auction DetailsDownload Terms Booklet

Exactly What We Handle

  • Legal Status Review

    Reviewing the title deed, restrictions and existing encumbrances on the property before any announcement.

  • Terms Booklet & Guarantee Deposit

    Drafting the terms, setting the opening price, guarantee deposit, payment mechanism and award deadline.

  • Announcement & Viewings

    Publishing the auction, responding to inquiries, and organizing field viewing appointments for interested parties.

  • Session Management & Award

    Managing the in-person or electronic auction, announcing the award, and documenting the result for all parties.

  • Post-Auction

    Following up on payment and title transfer procedures with the relevant authorities through to document handover.
